subroutine time_step_controller::time_step_controller_land ( class(time_step_controller_t), intent(inout)  this,
type(time_state_t), intent(inout)  time,
real(kind=dp), intent(in)  time_to_next 
)
private
Parameters
timeThe time state, whose dt is the step proposed by the CFL controller, adjusted in place.
time_to_nextThe time until the next scheduled time, as time_to_next of the controllers gives it, huge(0.0_dp) if none.

To be called right after set_dt. The time up to the next target is divided into the number of equal steps nearest, in ratio, to the proposed step, within min_dt_decrease_factor and max_dt_increase_factor of the previous step and below max_timestep. As long as the CFL controller keeps the step and the step divides the remaining time, which it does between scheduled times and at those of equal intervals, the step is returned unchanged. The end is the target if the next scheduled time is within min_dt_decrease_factor times the previous step of it, and that scheduled time is executed at the end. A scheduled time closer than that is passed, and executed at the first step past it.

Designed for output intervals of several steps, the same for all controllers or whole multiples of each other. Schedules that produce scheduled times close to each other make the step shorter than needed.
