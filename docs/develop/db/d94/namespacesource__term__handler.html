subroutine source_term_handler::source_term_handler_compute ( class(source_term_handler_t), intent(inout) this,
type(time_state_t), intent(in) time,
logical, intent(in) extrapolate,
type(field_t), intent(in), optional scale )
private

The source terms whose extrapolate attribute equals extrapolate are summed and multiplied by the mass matrix and, if present, by scale. For .true. the result replaces the right-hand side, for .false. it is added to it. A scheme that extrapolates its explicit terms in time calls the .true. form before and the .false. form after the extrapolation, so that the latter terms enter the step as computed.

Parameters
timeThe time state.
extrapolateSelect the terms by their extrapolate attribute.
scaleField multiplying the contributions, e.g. the density.
