We are pleased to announce that Neko will be well-represented at WCCM-ECCOMAS 2026. Members of our community and the core development team will be delivering several talks focused on the latest algorithmic advancements, performance optimizations, and scientific applications of the Neko framework.

If you are attending the conference, these sessions will provide an excellent opportunity to:

* **Discover** recent developments in Neko's architecture, including new features and GPU performance enhancements.
* **Explore** large-scale, real-world fluid dynamics applications powered by Neko.
* **Connect** with researchers, developers, and users to discuss the future of spectral element methods.

Please consult the official [WCCM-ECCOMAS conference program](https://wccm-eccomas2026.org) for the exact dates, times, and locations of the Neko-related sessions. We look forward to seeing you there and discussing how Neko can help accelerate your research.
